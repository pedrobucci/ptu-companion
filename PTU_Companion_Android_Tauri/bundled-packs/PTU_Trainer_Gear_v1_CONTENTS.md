# PTU Trainer Gear v1.0.0 — Contents

**Total:** 137 definitions — 98 equippable gear records and 39 supplied combat-item catalog records.  **Gear Store:** 84 purchasable equipment definitions.

Deterministic equipped bonuses are structured. Trigger/target/encounter-dependent rules remain contextual. Items requiring a Skill, Stat, Type or Biome use `equipment_config` on the equipped copy.

Artwork uses the PTU Unofficial/Serebii icon references supplied/verified for the catalog. The app renders those images directly and falls back to the category glyph if the image is unavailable. Pack-local `icon_asset_path` is also supported.
