## Automation notes

- Two-handed weapons use `hands: 2`, so the app reserves Off Hand while equipped.
- Normal Weapon Moves use normal weapon qualification and keep supported Feature substitutions such as Apparition.
- Arcane Weapons use Occult Education and Special attacks; this pack uses the PTU 1.05 Editation Adept/Master progression.
- Equip-only `compiled_effects` are added while equipped and removed on unequip.
- Static equipment bonuses such as Living Weapon Evasion are applied automatically.
- Triggered/target-dependent rules (critical hits, weakness, Scene-limited healing, shield-ready actions, etc.) are surfaced as contextual effects because they require combat-event state.
