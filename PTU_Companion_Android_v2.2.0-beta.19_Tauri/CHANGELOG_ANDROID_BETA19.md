# PTU Companion Android v2.2.0-beta.19

## Visual & navigation polish

- Pokédex & Rules now shows Pokémon portraits and item/definition artwork whenever available.
- Pokémon Progression · Stats adds **Adjusted Base** beside existing allocations, new points and final Stats.
- Pokémon Progression · Abilities is aligned and each selected Ability can open a resolved description modal.
- Pokémon Progression · Moves separates the checkbox from the clickable Move name; the name opens the full rule description.
- Roster and Storage selections expose **Open in Creatures**.
- Android bottom quick navigation replaces **Creatures** with **Trainer**; Creatures remains available under **More**.
