# PTU Companion Android v2.2.0-beta.15

## Hustle equipment mechanics

- Added deterministic support for **Hustle**: -2 to all Accuracy Rolls and +10 to all Damage Rolls.
- Equipment-granted Hustle from the Fine Large Sword now affects resolved Trainer Moves while the weapon is equipped.
- Accuracy is shown as a roll modifier rather than changing a Move's AC.
- Damage cards include the +10 bonus in their resolved damage expression and breakdown.
- Stats and Combat views show the active Accuracy and Damage modifiers.
- Unequipping the weapon removes Hustle and its modifiers automatically.
