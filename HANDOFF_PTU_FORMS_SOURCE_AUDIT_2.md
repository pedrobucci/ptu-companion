## Meloetta resolution

PTU Core 1.05, Relic Song, explicitly resolves the previously deferred Meloetta family:

- Meloetta must know Relic Song;
- it may switch between Aria Form and Step Form;
- switching is a Swift Action when Relic Song is being used;
- otherwise switching is a Standard Action;
- both Forms must use the same HP Stat.

Accordingly, Meloetta is now `transformation -> activeFormId` with `source_explicit` evidence. It was removed from the deferred queue and added to the supplemental source-rule catalog.
